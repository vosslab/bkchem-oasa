• Goal paused Objective: Take a deep look at the two primary components of this monorepo: the OASA chemistry backend and the
BKChem frontends. Over the past several months we have made substantial architectural progress, but many improvements remain
only partially completed or inconsistently integrated. The GUI is being rewritten from Tk to PySide6, and the new bkchem-qt.app
package already has real document sessions, tabs, modes, undo commands, and CDML round trips, but feature parity with the
classic BKChem application is still incomplete. Several dialog and configure paths continue to bypass the command and dirty-
state layer, and the legacy bkchem-app serves as the behavioral reference for completing the remaining functionality. The
backend/frontend boundary described in CDML_BACKEND_TO_FRONTEND_CONTRACT.md is likewise only partially migrated: OASA is
intended to own chemistry, the authoritative persistent document, and backend operations, while BKChem owns presentation,
interaction, and scene projection, with composition replacing inheritance. That boundary should become a clean, frontend-
agnostic contract based on stable document and chemistry interfaces rather than frontend-specific implementation details.
However, documented contract violations-including direct _vertices access, oasa.* classes in BKChem base types, and isinstance
checks against OASA graph classes-remain in the code. Coordinate generation has been consolidated onto the RDKit bridge, while
the Haworth renderer, render-operation unification, glyph-bond alignment, and packaging validation all remain partially
completed. The objective is to finish what we started and deliver a codebase that is clean, cohesive, contract-aligned, fully
validated, and at feature parity with the classic BKChem application, with new capabilities-including PubChem lookup, broader
import and export support, CDML-backed undo history, hex-grid alignment, and multi-ring Haworth rendering-built on that
completed foundation rather than around it. Time: 9h 9m.
